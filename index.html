Jarvis

A private, single-user personal assistant.

Jarvis is a personal productivity assistant built and used by Musabek Akmalov. It connects to the owner's own Google account with read-only access to help summarize email, review calendar events, and find information in Drive, Sheets, Docs, and YouTube.

Jarvis is not offered to the public and does not serve other users.
