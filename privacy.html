Privacy Policy

Jarvis personal assistant · Last updated: October 1, 2026

Who uses Jarvis

Jarvis is a private, single-user personal assistant operated by Musabek Akmalov for his own use. It is not offered to the public.

Data accessed

With the owner's consent through Google OAuth, Jarvis requests read-only access to the owner's Google account data in Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, Google Sheets, Google Docs, and YouTube. Jarvis does not create, modify, send, or delete any of this data.

How data is used

Data is used solely to provide assistant features to the owner, such as summaries, search, and reminders. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or training general-purpose AI models.

Sharing and sale

Data is not shared with, transferred to, or sold to any third party.

Storage and retention

Data is processed only as needed for the owner's requests and is kept only under the owner's control. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Jarvis's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
